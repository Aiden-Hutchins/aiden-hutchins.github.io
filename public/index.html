About

The investigative side of security.

I’m a cybersecurity student at Indiana Institute of Technology and a security analyst at Indiana Cyber Network, based in Fort Wayne, Indiana. Day to day I run vulnerability assessments and map client environments to NIST standards, but the work I’m drawn to is investigative: following public information to see what it actually reveals, and documenting it so someone else can verify every step.

That interest drives my independent OSINT work, including a dedicated investigation workstation built around Michael Bazzell’s methodology, and the public-facing guidance I write for the Indiana Executive Council on Cybersecurity. I’m working toward investigative and intelligence roles in government.
